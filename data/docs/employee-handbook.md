# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook (sample data)

This handbook is fictional sample data for testing the RAG pipeline.

## Working hours and remote work

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone. Employees may work fully remote, but must be reachable on the team chat during core hours. Teams hold a planning meeting every Monday and a demo every second Friday.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays in their country of residence. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March. Leave requests longer than 5 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 3 weeks in advance through the HR portal.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Expenses

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of purchase with an itemised receipt. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 45 USD per day. Software subscriptions above 100 USD per month need written approval from the engineering manager before purchase. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## Equipment

New engineers receive a laptop with 32 GB of RAM and a 1,000 USD home-office budget during their first 90 days. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours so they can be remotely wiped.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 USD for courses, books, conferences and certification exams. Unused budget does not roll over.
